Work together

If you'd like to make songs with me, leave your email. It comes to me as a Y3K inquiry.
 

I would like to work on songs with you. I think of songs as assets. It can take a lot of work to create these assets, but once you do every time someone uses them you make a tiny amount of money. Which, eventually, adds up.

If you are a DJ, or a singer or performer, when you play other people's songs you are advertising their assets. If you own the assets, every time you play a show you are advertising your own assets.

You have the chance to get people interested in your assets at an important time in their life, and they'll remember it forever. And they'll keep returning to that asset until they die.

But that said, it's hard out there making assets, I mean writing songs. I believe I can help.

What I'd like to do with you

The goal would be to work on a song, or a few songs, and make them good enough that you'd want to play them in your performances. That's when you are advertising the assets. Then, if you are out there performing the songs consistently, it's much easier to sell the songs or EP to a record label.

What I really like to do is record choir on songs, and I can do it quickly and cheaply. I kind of think of myself as a choir-liner, more than a top-liner. But basically I am just a music teacher.

I'm not a manager, and I really don't want to be. So I would only ever sign a small number of songs, or maybe even just one song. I hope you get a really good manager (if you don't have one already).

I really don't want to run a record label, but the deal I'd suggest is more like a record label deal. This production deal is basically aimed to eventually get the songs we make signed to a record label deal. Think of this as a step along the way.
