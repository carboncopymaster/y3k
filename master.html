The master is the actual recording of the song/s.

For a record label to be interested, they need a stake in the master. A future record label might want 50-70% of the master. So I'm just assuming 70%. Major labels would want more, indie labels might want less.

The rest of the master would then be split into thirds, with the artist getting 2 thirds and Y3K getting 1 third.
